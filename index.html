Naruto Complete Timeline

From Kaguya to the Seventh Hokage – Full Major Events

~1000 yrs ago

Kaguya arrives on Earth

Kaguya & Isshiki plant the God Tree. Kaguya betrays Isshiki, eats the Chakra Fruit and becomes the first chakra user.

~990 yrs ago

Birth of Hagoromo & Hamura + Sealing of Kaguya

The twins are born. Later they seal their mother (now the Ten-Tails) on the Moon. Black Zetsu is created.

~900 yrs ago

First Sharingan (Indra)

Indra Ōtsutsuki awakens the first true Sharingan. His descendants become the Uchiha clan.

~70 yrs ago

Founding of Konoha + 1st Hokage

Hashirama Senju & Madara Uchiha found Konohagakure. Hashirama is chosen as the First Hokage.

~65–60 yrs ago

Major battles: Madara vs Hashirama

Legendary repeated fights between the two. Ends with Madara’s “death” at the Valley of the End.

~55 yrs ago

Death of Izuna Uchiha

Izuna is fatally wounded by Tobirama. He gives his eyes to Madara, creating the first Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan.

~50–40 yrs ago

First Great Ninja War + 2nd Hokage

First world war. Tobirama becomes Second Hokage. He later sacrifices himself and appoints Hiruzen as Third Hokage.

~25–20 yrs ago

Second Great Ninja War

Jiraiya, Tsunade & Orochimaru become the Legendary Sannin. Dan Katō dies. Jiraiya meets Nagato, Yahiko & Konan.

~20 yrs ago

Death of Yahiko + Formation of Akatsuki

Yahiko is forced to kill himself. Nagato (as Pain) and Konan form the original Akatsuki to bring peace through force.

~13–10 yrs ago

Third Great Ninja War

War between the great nations. Minato becomes famous as the Yellow Flash of the Leaf.

~13 yrs ago

Death of Rin Nohara

Rin is made the Three-Tails jinchūriki. She dies on Kakashi’s Chidori to protect Konoha. Obito falls into despair and joins Madara while also unlocking Mangekyō Sharingan.

~10 yrs ago

Minato becomes 4th Hokage

After the Third War, Minato is appointed Fourth Hokage. Hiruzen retires.

Year 0

Birth of Naruto + Nine-Tails Attack

Naruto is born. Obito attacks with Kurama. Minato & Kushina die sealing the Nine-Tails into their son. Hiruzen returns as Hokage.

Age 7–8

Itachi awakens Mangekyō

Itachi witnesses Shisui’s suicide after Danzo steals one of his eyes. This trauma awakens Itachi’s Mangekyō Sharingan.

Age 7

Uchiha Clan Massacre

Itachi (with Obito) massacres the Uchiha clan under orders, sparing only Sasuke.

Age 12

Battle of the Great Naruto Bridge

Team 7’s first real mission. They defeat Zabuza & Haku. The bridge is named after Naruto.

Age 12–13

Orochimaru’s Invasion + Death of 3rd Hokage

Orochimaru invades during the Chūnin Exams. Hiruzen dies sealing Orochimaru’s arms with the Reaper Death Seal.

Age 13

Sasuke Retrieval Mission + Valley of the End

Sasuke leaves Konoha to join Orochimaru. Naruto chases him. They fight at the Valley of the End. Naruto fails to bring him back.

Age 13

Tsunade becomes 5th Hokage

After Hiruzen’s death, Tsunade is convinced by Jiraiya and Naruto to become the Fifth Hokage.

Age 15–16

Death of Jiraiya

Jiraiya infiltrates the Hidden Rain to investigate Pain. He dies after discovering Pain’s identity, leaving a coded message for Naruto.

Age 16

Itachi’s Death + Sasuke awakens Mangekyō

Sasuke kills Itachi. After learning the truth from Obito, the grief of losing his brother awakens Sasuke’s Mangekyō Sharingan.

Age 16

Sasuke obtains Eternal Mangekyō

Sasuke transplants Itachi’s eyes, gaining the Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan and unlocking Susanoo.

Age 16

Pain destroys Konoha

Nagato (Pain) completely destroys the village. Naruto returns and defeats him, becoming a hero.

Age 17

Death of Danzo (by Sasuke)

During the Five Kage Summit arc, Sasuke confronts and kills Danzo Shimura.

Age 17

Kabuto’s Edo Tensei – Revivals of Old Shinobi

Kabuto uses Impure World Reincarnation to revive hundreds of dead shinobi (previous Hokages, Itachi, Madara, Zabuza, Haku, Asuma, Deidara, Kakuzu, and many more) to fight in the Fourth Great Ninja War.

Age 17

Fourth Great Ninja War + Defeat of Kaguya

Allied Shinobi Forces vs Madara, Obito and the revived Kaguya. Naruto & Sasuke seal Kaguya. War ends on Naruto’s 17th birthday.

Age 17

Valley of the End – Final Battle

Right after the war, Naruto and Sasuke fight one last time at the Valley of the End. Both lose an arm. Sasuke is finally redeemed.

Age 17–18

Kakashi becomes 6th Hokage

After the war, Tsunade steps down. Kakashi is appointed Sixth Hokage.

Age 19

Naruto & Hinata marry

Two years after the war, Naruto and Hinata get married.

Age ~32

Naruto becomes 7th Hokage

Years later, Naruto finally achieves his lifelong dream and becomes the Seventh Hokage of Konohagakure.
